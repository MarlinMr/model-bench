      Four tricks that do all the work

      Nothing here is physics. It is a loaded brush, a steady wrist, and the patience to let paint meet paint while it's still wet.

      01

      Wet on wet

      The whole surface is pre-toned and still slick, so new colour slides into the old. That is why the sky has no edges — and why everything has to be painted in order.

      02

      The palette knife

      Mountains never see a brush. A knife loaded with sienna and blue, drawn downhill from the peak, gives rock, snow and shadow in one gesture.

      03

      The fan brush

      Load it with three greens, press, turn the wrist. Every tree, tuft and distant treeline here is a handful of those dabs.

      04

      Beat the devil out of it

      Halfway through, the brush is shaken against the easel. What spatters becomes wildflowers. No mistakes — only happy accidents.

      “Everyone needs a friend — even a painting. So the lake got ducks.”
